# Password and Multi-Factor Authentication Policy

Applies to: all employees and contractors, worldwide.

## Passwords

- Minimum length is 14 characters. Passphrases are encouraged.
- Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; they must be changed immediately if a compromise is suspected.
- Password managers approved by IT: the corporate password vault only.

## Multi-factor authentication

MFA is mandatory for every corporate account. Phishing-resistant methods (FIDO2 security keys or
Windows Hello for Business) are required for administrators.

## Reporting

Report suspected phishing to security@contoso.example within 1 hour of noticing it.
