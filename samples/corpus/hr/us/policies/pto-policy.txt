Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy - United States

Effective January 1, 2026.

Accrual
Full-time US employees accrue 20 days of PTO per calendar year (1.67 days per month). Part-time employees
accrue PTO pro rata to their scheduled hours.

Carry-over
Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on March 31.

Parental leave (US)
US employees are eligible for 16 weeks of paid parental leave after 90 days of employment.

Requests
PTO requests of more than 3 consecutive days require manager approval at least 2 weeks in advance.
