# United Kingdom Parental Leave Handbook

Applies to: employees of the UK entity. Owner: HR Operations (UK).

## Entitlement

UK employees with at least 26 weeks of continuous service are entitled to **26 weeks of fully paid parental
leave**, followed by up to 13 weeks of unpaid leave. This supersedes the entitlement in the group standard for
UK employees only.

## Notice

Employees must notify HR at least 15 weeks before the expected week of childbirth or placement, using the
Parental Leave Request form in the HR portal.

## Keeping-in-touch days

Up to 10 paid keeping-in-touch days may be worked during leave without ending the leave period.

## Return to work

Employees returning from parental leave may request flexible working for up to 12 months. Requests are answered
within 14 calendar days.
