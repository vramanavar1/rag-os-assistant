# Global Parental Leave Standard

Applies to: all employees worldwide, in every entity of the group. Owner: Group HR.

## Entitlement

Every employee with at least 12 months of continuous service is entitled to **18 weeks of fully paid parental
leave**, followed by up to 8 weeks of unpaid leave. The entitlement is the same for birth parents, adoptive
parents and surrogate parents.

## Local variation

Where a country handbook sets a higher entitlement than this standard, the local handbook applies. This
standard is the floor, never the ceiling.

## Notice

Employees should notify their people partner at least 12 weeks before the expected week of childbirth or
placement.

## Return to work

A phased return over the first 4 weeks is available on request.
