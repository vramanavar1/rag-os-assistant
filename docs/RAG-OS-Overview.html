Product overview

RAG-OS: the knowledge assistant that knows who is asking

Answers drawn only from your documents, only from the ones each person is allowed to see, with a page-level citation for every claim, and an honest "I don't know" when the documents don't say.

  • Version 1.0
  • Date 2 October 2026
  • Platform Microsoft Azure · Microsoft Foundry
  • GroundedEvery answer cites its source passages; uncited answers are refused.
  • Permission-awareDepartment, region, clearance and role are enforced inside the search.
  • YoursRuns in your Azure subscription, with models you choose.
  • AccountableToken cost and a correlation ID on every answer.

Contents

    1Executive summary

    Every organisation already holds the answers its people need. They sit in policies, price lists, SOPs, contracts and handbooks spread across HR, Sales, IT, Legal and Finance, and finding the right one, in its current version, is slow and error-prone.

    Generic AI assistants made that search feel instant, but they introduced two risks that enterprises cannot accept:

    • They leak. An assistant that can read everything will eventually quote a confidential salary band to an intern or a restricted contract clause to a partner.
    • They guess. When the documents do not cover a question, a language model still produces a fluent, plausible answer, and nobody can tell which parts are real.

    RAG-OS removes both risks by design. Each user's identity attributes (department, region, clearance level and role) are compiled into a filter that runs inside the search engine, so documents a person may not see are never retrieved, never sent to the model and never paraphrased. Answers are generated only from retrieved passages, carry numbered citations down to the page, and are refused outright when the evidence is missing.

    The business case

    Faster answers, fewer escalations

    Staff self-serve policy, pricing and process questions that today land on HR, Sales Ops, IT or Legal, with a citation they can open and verify.

    Lower risk of disclosure

    Access is enforced before generation, not filtered afterwards. The same assistant can safely serve an entire workforce, and even external portal users.

    Predictable, visible cost

    Embeddings run on self-hosted models with no per-token charge, and every answer reports its own token usage, so cost per question is measurable from day one.

    A platform, not a chatbot

    Once the knowledge base is indexed and classified, the same governed retrieval can power questionnaires, form filling and vertical workflows (see section 10).

    Why now

    Organisations have moved from AI pilots to production rollouts, and the questions buyers ask have changed accordingly: who can see what, where does the data go, can we prove where an answer came from and what does each answer cost. RAG-OS is built around exactly those questions, on the Azure and Microsoft Foundry estate many enterprises have already approved.

    2Introduction

    RAG-OS is an enterprise-grade knowledge-base assistant built on retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). It ingests an organisation's documents, classifies them against a vocabulary the organisation's own subject-matter experts control, indexes them for hybrid vector and keyword search, and answers questions in a chat interface, either its own or embedded in an existing intranet or customer portal.

    What sets it apart is that scope of visibility is part of the question. Two people asking the same thing may legitimately get different answers, because they are entitled to different documents:

    DimensionExample valuesHow it is matched
    DepartmentHR, Finance, Sales, IT, Legal, SupportThe user needs any one of the document's departments
    RegionGlobal › EMEA › UK, DE · AMER › US · APAC › INHierarchical: a UK user also sees EMEA and Global documents
    Clearance levelPublic, Internal, Confidential, RestrictedThe document's level must be at or below the user's
    RoleAdmin, taxonomy editor (SME), reviewer, contributorGoverns what a person can administer, classify or upload
    Personal shareOne document for one named personMatched on the user's directory object ID

    The attribute set is not hard-coded. It lives in a declarative access policy, so an organisation can add cost centre, legal entity, project or any other attribute without changing code.

    Who it is for: organisations of any size whose knowledge is spread across departments and must not be shared indiscriminately, starting with mid-sized enterprises on Microsoft Azure and growing toward SMBs through a subscription offering.

    3High-level product overview

    RAG-OS has two independent paths that meet at a shared search index. The ingestion path turns documents into classified, searchable passages; it runs on its own compute and scales from zero to many workers without affecting users. The query path turns a signed-in user's question into a cited answer drawn only from passages that user is entitled to see.

    3.2 User query path

    1. Sign in. The user signs in with Microsoft Entra ID, in the RAG-OS chat UI or in a host portal that embeds it with a single script tag.
    2. Resolve scope. The token's claims are mapped to the user's department, region, clearance and roles. Anything missing defaults to deny.
    3. Filter inside the search. The access policy is compiled into a search filter and applied inside Azure AI Search, so only permitted passages are ever candidates.
    4. Retrieve and rank. Hybrid vector and keyword retrieval is followed by semantic re-ranking.
    5. Answer or abstain. The language model answers only from the retrieved passages and cites them as [n]. If there is nothing relevant, or the answer would be uncited, RAG-OS declines and says why.

    3.3 Answer quality scenarios

    Four behaviours separate a trustworthy assistant from a plausible one. Each is stated with its current status.

    Version correctness Partial

    When a document is re-ingested, its previous version is removed from the index, so stale copies of the same document are never quoted.

    Choosing between different documents (for example ProductPriceCatalog_2025 vs ProductPriceCatalog_2026) currently relies on an instruction to prefer the most recent effective date. The effective date is read from Markdown and text metadata today.

    Roadmap: effective dates for every format, version-aware ranking, and an explicit "as of" filter in the query.

    Page-level citation Available

    Every answer carries numbered citations with the document, the page number (for PDFs) or section heading, a snippet and a relevance score.

    When the same passage appears in several files, the citation lists the other locations too, instead of hiding them.

    Example: "Parental leave is 26 weeks [1]." → [1] UK Parental Leave Handbook, page 4.

    Abstention Available

    RAG-OS returns no answer rather than a plausible one, with a machine-readable reason:

    • no_access: nothing this user may see
    • no_relevant_context: the corpus does not cover it
    • model_refusal: the model declined
    • Grounding guard: an answer without citations is discarded

    Conflict handling Partial

    When two permitted sources disagree, for example a global and a UK handbook, the answer surfaces the conflict and cites both, rather than choosing silently.

    A user who can see only one of the two sources gets a clean answer from that source, because the other was never retrieved.

    Roadmap: a structured conflict flag in the API response, so portals and workflows can act on it.

    The embedding-profile guarantee. Exactly one embedding profile is active at a time. It pins the model, revision, dimensions and query/document prefixes, and both ingestion and querying read the same profile. A mismatch between how documents and questions are embedded, a common and silent cause of poor RAG quality, cannot happen. Changing the profile builds a new index alongside the old one, which keeps serving until the new one is ready.

    5Secure data querying

    Users are governed by Microsoft Entra ID: app roles decide what a person can do, and directory attributes decide what a person can see. Documents carry matching facets (tags) in the index. A declarative policy joins the two.

    Clearance levels

    0PublicAnyone signed in. Also the default when a document has no clearance.
    1InternalEmployees, within their department and region.
    2ConfidentialNamed roles or teams with a business need.
    3RestrictedThe narrowest audience: legal, board or regulated data.

    Levels are configurable in the access policy, so an organisation that needs an extra tier, such as "Private", can add one.

    Pre-filtering, not post-filtering

    Many assistants retrieve broadly and then hide what the user should not see. RAG-OS never retrieves it. The policy (default deny; department and region and clearance, or a personal share) is compiled into a filter that the search engine applies inside every query. Restricted text never reaches the model, the logs or the answer.

    6Data compliance

    For regulated organisations the first question is not "how good are the answers" but "where does our data go". RAG-OS is designed to keep the answer to that question short.

    Your tenant, your subscription Available

    RAG-OS deploys into the customer's own Azure subscription. Documents, index, database, logs and keys stay under the customer's control and policies.

    Embeddings never leave Available

    With the default self-hosted embedding model, document text is vectorised inside the deployment. No document content is sent to a third-party embedding API.

    Minimal exposure to the LLM Available

    Only the handful of passages the user is already entitled to see are sent to the answering model, through Microsoft Foundry or Azure OpenAI in the customer's chosen region.

    Compliance-perimeter models Roadmap

    Answering models running on-premises or on Azure confidential-compute infrastructure, so that even the answering step stays inside a defined perimeter.

    7Observability and operations

    Tokens per answer Available

    Every answer reports input, output, cache and embedding tokens, split by purpose, and the same figures are emitted as metrics by provider and model.

    End-to-end tracing Available

    A correlation ID follows a request from the browser through the API, and follows a document from upload through queue, worker and index. OpenTelemetry exports to Azure Application Insights.

    Ready-made queries Available

    A library of KQL queries for traces, token dashboards, failures and alerts ships with the product.

    Admin console Available

    Dashboard, sources and "sync now", ingestion runs with progress and ETA, per-document status, bulk retry, CSV export, SME review queue, dead-letter viewer, ingestion throttling and configuration editing.

    10Potential use cases

    The core product is a governed question-answering service. Because the knowledge base is indexed, classified and permission-tagged, the same foundation supports a family of higher-value applications.

    10.1 Knowledge-base assistant for SMBs (SaaS subscription) Core available SaaS roadmap

    Most SMBs keep their standard operating procedures across HR, Sales, IT, Legal, Finance and Training in scattered folders. RAG-OS makes them queryable, with reliable, cited answers and department-level privacy, as a subscription that needs no AI expertise to run.

    • Packaging: tiers by seats and document volume; usage-based add-ons, metered by the token figures the product already records.
    • Onboarding: connect a folder or storage account, apply a starter taxonomy, and invite users.

    10.2 Questionnaire generation for multiple verticals Roadmap

    A searchable, classified knowledge base can be turned into assessments as well as answers. Every generated question is grounded in, and cites, a source passage.

    Question types

    • Objective (multiple choice)
    • Fill in the blanks
    • Match the columns
    • Descriptive / open questions

    Verticals

    • Education: quizzes and exams from syllabus material
    • Hiring: online and offline interview sets from role profiles
    • Compliance and training certification
    • Fun quizzes and engagement

    Evaluation

    Objective questions are marked automatically. Descriptive answers are scored against the cited source passages, and the evaluator's confidence decides how marks are allocated; low-confidence results go to a human reviewer.

    10.3 Form filling Roadmap

    Use the indexed knowledge base to pre-fill structured forms, with a citation behind every value so a person can verify it before submitting.

    • Legal forms and standard agreements
    • Tax forms
    • Application forms: licences, permits, grants, admissions, visas
    • Custom templated reports for compliance and audits

    10.4 Personal assistants Roadmap

    Individuals keep educational, financial, employment, property and household documents in one private, searchable space ("When does my home insurance renew?", "What was my gross salary in 2025?"). Combined with form filling, the personal knowledge base becomes the data source for everyday paperwork.

    10.5 Niche verticals: finance, insurance, healthcare Roadmap

    The highest-value opportunities are narrow, rule-heavy processes where checking documents against a specific version of a specific guideline consumes skilled staff time. The same pattern applies to insurance claims, KYC and onboarding files, clinical prior-authorisation packets and grant applications. Subject-matter experts are the ones who spot these niches; RAG-OS gives them the tools to encode their judgement.

    Worked example: mortgage loss-mitigation document chase. A homeowner falls behind on payments and applies for hardship assistance. The servicer must decide whether the submitted package is complete against the relevant investor guideline, then state precisely what is missing. The judgement is completeness against a specific version of a specific guide.

    How RAG-OS addresses it:

    1. Versioned guideline corpus. Each investor guide is indexed with its effective dates, so the check runs against the version in force for the application.
    2. Taxonomy. SMEs define the document types: hardship statement, proof of income, bank statements, tax records, property and occupancy evidence.
    3. Ontology. SMEs define the relationships: guideline version → requires → document type, with conditions (for example, a self-employed borrower requires profit-and-loss evidence) and validity rules such as how recent a statement must be.
    4. Completeness agent. The agent classifies the submitted package, evaluates it against the ontology for that guideline version, and produces a missing-items list where every requirement cites the guideline page it comes from.

    11Future scope and potential

    The roadmap moves in three steps: complete the enterprise product, then open the platform, then build workflows on top of it.

    Next

    Complete the enterprise product

    • SharePoint connector with permission sync, then Google Drive and SFTP
    • OCR for scanned documents (Azure Content Understanding)
    • Evaluation pipeline (RAGAS, DeepEval, Azure AI Evaluation) as CI/CD release gates
    • Version-aware ranking, and structured conflict and "as of" signals
    • UI editors for taxonomy and access policy
    • Streaming answers; Bicep templates; private endpoints and VNet

    Later

    Open the platform

    • Multi-tenant SaaS control plane: tenant isolation, per-tenant configuration, metering and billing on recorded token usage
    • On-premises and confidential-compute model adapters
    • Publish as a Foundry agent for Teams and Copilot Studio; expose governed retrieval as an MCP server
    • Foundry IQ knowledge bases as an alternative retrieval engine
    • Purview sensitivity-label mapping; semantic cache and an API-gateway token budget

    Horizon

    Workflows on governed knowledge

    • Questionnaire generation and grading
    • Citation-backed form filling
    • Completeness-checking agents for regulated verticals
    • Ontology and knowledge-graph reasoning across documents
    • A marketplace of domain packs (taxonomy, ontology and evaluation sets)
    • Personal knowledge assistant edition

    Market potential

    Three revenue streams build on one platform:

    • Dedicated enterprise deployments for regulated organisations that need the product inside their own subscription.
    • SaaS subscriptions for SMBs that need a trustworthy SOP assistant without an AI team.
    • Vertical solutions (domain packs and workflow agents) priced on the effort they save, such as hours of document chasing per mortgage file.

    B. Glossary

    RAGRetrieval-augmented generation: answering with a language model from passages retrieved at question time, rather than from the model's memory.
    ABAC / RBACAttribute-based and role-based access control. RAG-OS uses roles for what a person can do and attributes for what a person can see.
    FacetA controlled tag on a document, such as department, region or clearance, used for filtering and classification.
    TaxonomyThe SME-owned vocabulary of facets, values, synonyms and hierarchies.
    OntologyThe relationships between concepts in a domain, for example "guideline version requires document type".
    Embedding profileThe pinned model, revision, dimensions and prefixes used for both ingestion and queries.
    Pre-filterAn access filter applied inside the search query, so that forbidden content is never retrieved.
    AbstentionDeclining to answer, with a reason, when the permitted evidence does not support an answer.
    MCPModel Context Protocol: an open standard for connecting AI agents to tools and data.
